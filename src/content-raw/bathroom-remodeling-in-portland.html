Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long does a bathroom remodel in Portland, OR typically take?

The timeline depends on the size and scope of your project. On average, a full bathroom remodel in Portland can take 3–6 weeks, including demolition, plumbing, electrical, tiling, and fixture installation. We’ll provide you with a clear schedule before the project begins so you know what to expect.

2. Can I use my bathroom during the remodel?

In most cases, your bathroom will be out of service while work is underway. If your home has multiple bathrooms, we’ll make sure the remodel causes as little disruption as possible. For single-bathroom homes in Portland, we can discuss temporary solutions to ensure your household’s comfort during construction.

3. Do I need to move out during the renovation?

Most clients remain at home. We’ll minimize disruption and maintain a clean work environment.

4. What types of finishes and fixtures do you offer?

We offer a wide variety of premium vanities, tiles, faucets, and lighting options.

5. Do you provide waterproofing and mold protection?

Absolutely. Waterproofing and mold-resistant materials are standard in our bathroom remodels.
