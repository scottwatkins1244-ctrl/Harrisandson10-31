4 Things to Look Out For When Hiring a Construction Company
  • Christian Harris
  • June 3, 2024
  • 7:00 am
  • 5 Minutes Read

Table of Contents

Embarking on a construction project, whether a minor home renovation or a substantial commercial build, demands the expertise of a reliable construction company. The significance of selecting the right contractor cannot be overstated, as the wrong choice can result in delays, cost overruns, and substandard workmanship. To ensure your project’s success and peace of mind, consider these four crucial factors when hiring a construction company. With Harris & Sons, you can be confident that we embody these essential qualities, providing exceptional service and a commitment to excellence. Dive into these key considerations to make an informed decision and learn how Harris & Sons can be your trusted partner in construction.

1. Check Their Insurance Coverage

One of the most crucial steps in hiring a construction company is verifying their insurance coverage. This includes checking for both liability insurance and workers’ compensation insurance.

Importance of Contractor Insurance

Insurance is a safeguard against potential legal and financial issues that can arise during a construction project. Liability insurance protects you from lawsuits related to injuries or property damage that occur on your property during the project. Workers’ compensation insurance ensures that you are not held liable for any injuries that workers may sustain while on the job.

Verifying Insurance Coverage

To ensure the contractor is adequately insured, ask for copies of their insurance certificates. Confirm that their policies are current and cover the scope of your project. It’s also wise to contact the insurance provider to verify the coverage.

Insurance Checklist for Contractors

  • Liability Insurance: Covers property damage and injury claims.
  • Workers’ Compensation Insurance: Protects you from liability for worker injuries.
  • Additional Coverage: Depending on your project, you might need to check for additional types of insurance, such as builder’s risk insurance.

For example, if you’re in Hillsboro, OR, or Happy Valley, OR, local regulations may have specific insurance requirements for construction companies. Ensure your contractor complies with these regulations.

2. Do They Have Any Experience with Your Project?

Experience matters greatly in the construction industry. Hiring a contractor with relevant experience can make a significant difference in the quality and efficiency of your project.

Evaluating Contractor Project Experience

Ask potential contractors about their experience with projects similar to yours. This can include the type of construction, scale, and any specific challenges related to your project. For instance, if you’re planning a commercial build in Lake Oswego, OR, you’ll want a contractor with a strong portfolio in commercial construction.

Checking Contractor’s Past Projects

Request a portfolio of their previous work and, if possible, visit some of these sites. Speak with past clients to get a sense of the contractor’s reliability, work quality, and professionalism.

Contractor Specialization

Some contractors specialize in specific types of projects, such as residential homes, commercial buildings, or specific renovations like kitchen remodels. Ensure the contractor you choose has a proven track record in the type of project you’re planning.

3. Does the Contract Include Everything That’s Needed?

A comprehensive contract is essential for avoiding misunderstandings and ensuring that all aspects of the project are clearly defined.

Construction Contract Essentials

The contract should outline the scope of work, including detailed descriptions of each phase of the project, materials to be used, timelines, and payment terms. It should also specify what happens in the event of delays or changes to the project scope.

What to Include in a Contractor Agreement

  • Scope of Work: Detailed description of tasks and responsibilities.
  • Payment Terms: Schedule of payments and total cost.
  • Timeline: Start and completion dates.
  • Materials and Equipment: Specifications for all materials and equipment to be used.
  • Change Orders: Process for handling changes to the project scope or materials.
  • Termination Clause: Conditions under which the contract can be terminated.

Reviewing Construction Contracts

Carefully review the contract before signing. If needed, seek legal advice to ensure that all terms are clear and fair. A well-drafted contract protects both parties and helps ensure the project runs smoothly.

4. Ask About Previous Work Experience

A contractor’s past performance can be a good indicator of their future performance on your project. Ask for references and check their work history thoroughly.

Contractor References and Past Work

Contact past clients to ask about their experience with the contractor. Important questions include:

  • Were they satisfied with the quality of work?
  • Was the project completed on time and within budget?
  • How did the contractor handle any issues that arose?

Contractor Project Portfolio

A strong project portfolio showcases the contractor’s capabilities and gives you an idea of their style and quality of work. Look for diversity in their projects, as this can indicate flexibility and a broad skill set.

Checking Contractor’s Past Projects

Sites like Houzz, Angi, or Yelp can provide reviews and ratings of contractors. Additionally, some local government websites in places like Tigard, OR, or
Happy Valley, OR, may list licensed contractors and their performance records.

Conclusion

Hiring a construction company is a crucial decision that can significantly influence the success of your project. By concentrating on four key factors—insurance coverage, relevant experience, comprehensive contracts, and past work experience—you can sidestep common issues and ensure your project meets your highest standards.

When you’re ready to hire a construction company in places like Happy Valley, OR, Gresham, OR, these tips will guide you toward an informed decision. Conducting thorough research upfront can save you time, money, and stress. For those planning anything from minor renovations to major builds, selecting the right contractor is the foundational step to a successful project.
